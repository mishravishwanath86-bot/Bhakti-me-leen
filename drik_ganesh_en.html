Lord Ganesh Aarti

Ganesha Visarjan - Sep 25, Fri
Aarti, Puja Vidhi, Chalisa, Ganesha Names
Anant Chaturdashi, Lord Ganesha, Rangolis

Traditionally Lord Ganesh, son of Mahadev Shiva, is worshipped first among all Gods. Lord Ganesh bestows wisdom and removes all obstacles which might come while performing auspicious work. Hence Lord Ganesh is worshipped first while starting all Pujas and auspicious activities.

Jay Ganesh Deva is one of the most popular and charming Aartis among all devotional Aartis. Aarti Gajavadan Vinayaka Ki is another popular Aarti of Lord Ganesha.

Lord Ganesha

Lord Ganesha is one of the most popular deities in Hindu Dharma. He is revered as Pratham Pujya i.e. He is worshipped first among all the deities. He is worshipped to remove all kinds of obstacles.

Ganesha Chaturthi

According to Hindu calendar, Shukla Paksha Vinayaka Chaturthi of the Bhadrapada month is known as Ganesha Chaturthi. The birth anniversary of Lord Ganesha is celebrated as Ganesha Chaturthi. Lord Ganesha is worshipped as the God of wisdom, prosperity and good fortune.

Kerala Ganesha Chaturthi

Kerala Ganesha Chaturthi is celebrated on Shukla Paksha Chaturthi in Chingam Masam as per the Malayalam Calendar. People worship Lord Ganesha on this day.

108 Ganesha Names

1. Om Gajananaya Namah। 2. Om Ganadhyakshaya Namah। 3. Om Vighnarajaya Namah। ... 107. Om Akrantachidachitprabhave Namah। 108. Om Shri Vighneshvaraya Namah। Ashtottara Shatanamavali of Ganesha is a collection of 108 divine names of Lord Ganesha. One can recite these names to seek the blessings of Lord Ganesha.

Ganesha Chalisa

'Ganesha Chalisa' is a hymn composed of 40 Chaupais in the praise of Lord Ganesha. One can get rid of all the obstacles by reciting Ganesha Chalisa.

Ganesha Visarjan

On Ganesha Visarjana day the idol of Lord Ganesha is immersed in water body, usually in lakes or ponds after a large procession. Ananta Chaturdashi is considered the most important day for Ganesha Visarjana.

Ashta Vinayaka

The divine eight incarnations of Lord Ganesha are collectively known as Ashta Vinayaka. Vakratunda, Ekadanta, Mahodara and Gajanana are some popular forms of Ashta Vinayaka.
