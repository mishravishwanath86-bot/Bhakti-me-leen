Lord Gangadhara Aarti

Om Jai Gangadhara is one of the most famous Aarti of Lord Shiva.

हिEnঅবাગુಕമଓதతె
X

॥ भगवान गङ्गाधर आरती ॥

ॐ जय गङ्गाधर जय हर जय गिरिजाधीशा।

त्वं मां पालय नित्यं कृपया जगदीशा॥

ॐ हर हर हर महादेव॥

कैलासे गिरिशिखरे कल्पद्रुमविपिने।

गुन्जति मधुकरपुन्जे कुन्जवने गहने॥

कोकिलकूजित खेलत हन्सावन ललिता।

रचयति कलाकलापं नृत्यति मुदसहिता॥

ॐ हर हर हर महादेव॥

तस्मिन्ल्ललितसुदेशे शाला मणिरचिता।

तन्मध्ये हरनिकटे गौरी मुदसहिता॥

क्रीडा रचयति भुषारज्जित निजमीशम्।

इन्द्रादिक सुर सेवत नामयते शीशम्॥

ॐ हर हर हर महादेव॥

बिबुधबधू बहु नृत्यत हृदये मुदसहिता।

किन्नर गायन कुरुते सप्त स्वरसहिता॥

धिनकत थै थै धिनकत मृदङ्ग वादयते।

क्वण क्वण ललिता वेणुं मधुरं नाटयते॥

ॐ हर हर हर महादेव॥

रुण रुण चरणे रचयति नूपुरमुज्ज्वलिता।

चक्रावर्ते भ्रमयति कुरुते तां धिक तां॥

तां तां लुप चुप तां तां डमरू वादयते।

अङ्गुष्ठांगुलिनादं लासकतां कुरुते॥

ॐ हर हर हर महादेव॥

कर्पूरघुतिगौरं पन्चाननसहितम्।

त्रिनयनशशिधरमौलिं विषधरकण्ठयुतम्॥

सुन्दरजटायकलापं पावकयुतभालम्।

डमरुत्रिशूलपिनाकं करधृतनृकपालम्॥

ॐ हर हर हर महादेव॥

मुण्डै रचयति माला पन्नगमुपवीतम्।

वामविभागे गिरिजारूपं अतिललितम्॥

सुन्दरसकलशरीरे कृतभस्माभरणम्।

इति वृषभध्वजरूपं तापत्रयहरणम्॥

ॐ हर हर हर महादेव॥

शङ्खनिनदम् कृत्वा झल्लरि नादयते।

नीराजयते ब्रह्मा वेद-ऋचां पठते॥

अतिमृदुचरणसरोजं हृत्कमले धृत्वा।

अवलोकयति महेशं ईशं अभिनत्वा॥

ॐ हर हर हर महादेव॥

ध्यानं आरति समये हृदये अति कृत्वा।

रामस्त्रिजटानाथं ईशं अभिनत्वा॥

सन्गतिमेवं प्रतिदिन पठनं यः कुरुते।

शिवसायुज्यं गच्छति भक्त्या यः श्रृणुते॥

ॐ हर हर हर महादेव॥

Maha Shivaratri

Lord Shiva is worshipped on Maha Shivaratri day. It is believed that Lord Shiva was born as Linga at midnight on this day. Lord Shiva is worshipped in all the four Prahar of the night on Maha Shivaratri. Nishita Kaal Puja Time for Maha Shivaratri is given on this page.

Lord Shiva

Lord Shiva is one of the three main deities worshipped in Hinduism, namely Brahma, Vishnu & Mahesha, who are collectively known as Trimurti. Lord Shiva is the destroyer of the universe and is also known by the names of Bholenath, Lord Shankara, etc.

108 Shiva Names

108 Shiva Names mentioned in Shakta Pramod Grantha are the most popular 108 Names of Lord Shiva. Each name when combined with Om Namah becomes independent Mantra in itself and can be used for chanting as well as to perform Shiva Ashtottara Shatanamavali Homa.

Lord Shiva Chalisa

Jai Girija Pati Dinadayala। Sada Karata Santana Pratipala॥ Bhala Chandrama Sohata Nike। Kanana Kundala Nagaphani Ke॥ Lord Shiva Chalisa is provided with lyrics and recitation video. Devotees of Lord Shiva recite this Chalisa to please Him and seek His blessings.

Lord Mahadev Aarti

Satya, Sanatana, Sundara, Shiva Sabake Swami। Avikari Avinashi, Aja Antaryami॥ Hara Hara Hara Mahadeva! Lord Mahadev Aarti is given with lyrics and recitation video. It is a very popular Aarti dedicated to the Mahadeva form of Lord Shiva.

Lord Shankara Aarti

Jayati Jayati Jaga-Nivasa, Shankara Sukhakari॥ Ajara Amara Aja Arupa, Sata Chita Anandarupa। Vyapaka Brahmaswarupa, Bhava! Bhava-Bhaya-Hari॥ Aarti of Lord Shankar is given with lyrics and recitation video. Lord Shankar is one of the three main deities worshipped in Hinduism, known as Trimurti.

Lord Kailasavasi Aarti

Shisha Ganga Ardhanga Parvati Sada Virajata Kailasi। Nandi Bhringi Nritya Karata Hain, Dharata Dhyana Sura Sukharasi॥ The above Lord Kailashvasi Aarti is given with lyrics and recitation video. It's a very melodious Aarti dedicated to the Kailashvasi form of Lord Shiva.

Shiva Puja Vidhi

A detailed Shodashopachara Shiva Puja Vidhi dedicated to Lord Shiva is provided. In the presented Puja Vidhi, all the sixteen steps of Shodashopachara Puja Vidhi including Dhyana, Avahana, Padya, Arghya, and Achamaniya are described with Mantras.

Shivaratri Puja Vidhi

Shivaratri Shodashopachara Puja Vidhi consisting of all the main steps of worship like Sankalpa, Dhyana, Avahana, Asana, Arghya etc. has been provided along with Mantra. With the help of this Puja Vidhi, you can take full advantage of Maha Shivaratri by performing worship in a proper manner.

Shivaratri Greeting

Maha Shivaratri Greeting Cards with pictures of Shiva Mukha Lingam, Swayambhu Shiva Lingam Abhishek, Lord Shiva on Mount Kailash and Shiva Grinding Bhang are given. You can send Maha Shivaratri greetings to your loved ones through these beautiful pictures.

Masik Shivaratri

All Masik Shivaratri that occur during a year are listed. According to the Hindu calendar, Krishna Paksha Chaturdashi of every month is celebrated as Masik Shivaratri. Devotees of Lord Shiva observe a one-day fast on this occasion.

Sawan Somwar

All Mondays or Somwar which fall during Shravana month are considered highly auspicious for fasting to seek blessing of Lord Shiva and known as Shravana Somwar. Most states follow regional calendars due to which dates of Sawan Somwar might differ by 2 weeks.

Sawan Shivaratri

In North India, Shivaratri which falls during the auspicious month of Shravana is known as Sawan Shivaratri. Except Hindi belt in North India, in rest of India Sawan begins one or two days after Sawan Shivaratri.

Pradosham Dates

Pradosh Vrat, also known as Pradosham in South India, is observed to seek blessings of Lord Shiva. According to the Hindu calendar, this fast is observed on the Trayodashi Tithi of both Shukla and Krishna Paksha of the lunar month. Pradosham days for whole year are listed on this page.

Shiva Rangoli Designs

Various types of Lord Shiva Rangoli designs and Shiva Linga Rangoli designs are given. The method of making beautiful Rangoli is also described step by step through pictures. With the help of these pictures, one can make Rangoli very easily.

Shiva Mantras

Om Namah Shivaya i.e. the Mula Mantra of Lord Shiva is the most powerful Mantra of Lord Shiva. Om Tryambakam, the death defeating Mantra of Lord Shiva. Om Tatpurushaya, the Rudra Gayatri Mantra of Lord Shiva are few powerful Mantra to please and seek blessings of Lord Shiva.

Shiva Ramashtakam

Shivahare Shivarama Sakhe Prabho, Trividhatapa-nivarana He Vibho। Aja Janeshvara Yadava Pahi Mam, Shiva Hare Vijayam Kuru Me Varam॥1॥ Shiva Ramashtakam is given with lyrics and recitation video. It is a very melodious and effective Ashtakam dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Shri Shiva Ashtottarashatanama Stotram

Shivo Maheshvarah Shambhuh Pinaki Shashishekharah। Vamadevo Virupakshah Kapardi Nilalohitah॥1॥ Shri Shiva Ashtottarashatanama Stotram is given with lyrics and video. Reciting this Stotram is considered to be very auspicious and fruitful.
